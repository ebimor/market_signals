  GET /api/market/watchlists                - List all watchlists

  DELETE /api/market/watchlist/{id}         - Delete watchlist
  POST /api/market/watchlist/{id}/items     - Add ticker to watchlist
  DELETE /api/market/watchlist/{id}/items/{ticker} - Remove ticker

Signals & Analytics:
  GET /api/market/signals                   - Get trading signals
  GET /api/market/stats                     - Get statistics
  GET /api/market/stocks                    - List all stocks
  GET /api/market/stocks/{ticker}           - Get stock details
  POST /api/market/trades                   - Record trade
  GET /api/market/trades                    - Get trade history
